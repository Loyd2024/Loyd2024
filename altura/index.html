The address

The heart of Upper Hill. Finally, a place to live.

Upper Hill is home to Nairobi Hospital, Kenyatta National Hospital, the World Bank, the IMF, embassies and the head offices of CIC and Britam, yet it has very few homes. Altura changes that with 250 residences, three levels of retail and a full floor of private medical suites.

Medical professionals

Walk to Nairobi Hospital. Consult from the private medical suites downstairs. Be home minutes after your last round.

Diplomats & expatriates

Secure, fully serviced living close to embassies, multilaterals and head offices, with a reception and manned security.

Investors

Year-round demand from patients and their families, visiting specialists and corporate tenants in a district short on homes.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Altura complete?

Yes. Altura is complete and viewings are open. Book a slot through the form above to tour the residences and the rooftop in person, or ask for a live video walkthrough.

Can I buy from abroad or as a non-Kenyan?

Yes. Kenyans in the diaspora and foreign nationals can buy apartments in Kenya, and much of the process can be completed remotely. Our team will walk you through the documentation, title and payment steps.

Are payment plans or mortgages available?

Ask for the Altura pack and we'll include the current payment plans and mortgage options alongside the price list, so you can compare them side by side.

Is Altura a good rental investment?

Upper Hill combines major hospitals, embassies, multilaterals and corporate head offices with very little residential supply, which supports demand for both short-stay and long-term lets. Try the returns estimator above, then ask our team for current rental data.

What amenities do residents get?

A rooftop infinity pool, sky lounge, rooftop gardens, wellness lounge, gym and spa, plus a laundry room on every floor, borehole water, backup power, high-speed lifts, CCTV, manned security and a ground-floor reception.
