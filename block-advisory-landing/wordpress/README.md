# block.ke: development property page and payment calculators

`block-development-page.php` is a single WPCode PHP snippet that improves block.ke property pages in four ways. Everything it shows is read from the listing, so a development keeps working like any other block.ke property: same URL, site header and menu, search and maps, agent, similar listings and SEO. To update the page, edit the property as usual.

## 1. Payment calculators on every for-sale property page

| Listing | Calculator |
|---|---|
| **Off-plan**: Property Status *Off-Plan / Ongoing* and Completion not *Ready now* | **Payment plan calculator**: deposit, then monthly or quarterly instalments up to completion. It replaces the theme's mortgage calculator in the same place on the page. |
| **Complete**: Status *Complete*, or Completion *Ready now* | The theme's mortgage calculator, unchanged. |

The instalment calculator fills itself in from the listing:

- **Price:** the listing price. Visitors can change it.
- **Deposit and reservation fee:** read from the description's *Payment Plan* section, for example "Deposit: 20% of the purchase price" and "Reservation fee: KES 200,000". If the listing states no deposit, the calculator uses 20% and says so.
- **Months:** from today until the completion date. The date comes from `bke_completion_stated`, a "Completion" or "Occupation" line in the description, or the Completion year. With no date, visitors choose a period of 6 to 60 months.

Its button opens the agent contact form with "Please send me the official payment plan" already typed in.

**Mortgage defaults need updating.** The theme's mortgage calculator currently assumes 4.125% interest over 30 years, which are US figures. Update them in *WpResidence Options → Property Page → Mortgage calculator*, for example to 13% over 20 years.

## 2. Amenities and nearby places on every property page

These two changes apply to the theme's own (classic) property pages.

**Features & Amenities** opens with up to six amenity tiles, as on the development layout: navy tiles with a brass icon, the amenity's name and a one-line description. Everything else follows as one checklist, titled "The everyday essentials" when it holds only security, parking, lifts, water and power, otherwise "Also included". This replaces the theme's list of feature groups, where most groups held one to three items under their own heading. The items are the listing's *Features & Amenities* ticks, as before, so there is nothing new to fill in.

**Address** gains a *What's nearby* panel. It lists the nearest two business districts, malls, schools or universities, hospitals, parks and airports, with straight-line distances from the listing's map pin. The places are a curated list of 36 Nairobi landmarks in `blockke_dev_landmarks()`, and more can be added there, for example Gateway Mall for Syokimau listings. A group appears only when one of its places is close enough to matter: 6 km for malls and parks, 8 km for schools and hospitals (5 km for universities), and further for business districts and airports. There is no panel for listings outside Nairobi, without a map pin, or with the pin left on the theme's default map centre. To show your own list instead, fill in **Places nearby** in the listing's *Development page* box, one per line as `Place | travel time`.

Both are moved into place by a small script at the end of the page. Without JavaScript, or if the theme's markup changes, the theme's own content stays as it was.

## 3. Development layout (switched on per listing)

A landing-page layout for new developments, drawn between the site's normal header and footer.

| Section | Comes from |

| Hero: name, area, status, *From* price, completion, photo, two-field enquiry form | Title, Area, Property Status, lowest unit price, completion date, featured image. A ready single home reads *Price*, unless its price label ("before" label) is *From*. An off-plan villa or house at one fixed price reads *Price* too, unless its text says "from KES …" or gives a range. Off-plan apartments, studios and offices keep *From*. |
| Key facts strip | The description's *at a Glance* or *Project Snapshot* table or list, plus unit sizes. A single home (a unit page or a one-home listing) also shows its bedrooms, bathrooms, built area and plot (in m², like the sizes). |
| Overview and *Read the full description* | The description's opening paragraphs and its *Why …* list. A short title-like first line ("3 Bedroom Villa at Tilisi Views, Limuru") is left out, since the page heading already says it. The full description stays on the page, collapsed; on phones its wide tables swipe sideways, with a shadow on the side that has more. |
| Photos | The listing gallery, with each photo's Media Library caption when it has one |
| Residences table, with filters | The description's Unit / Size / Price table (a price column may also be headed *From* or *Starting*), linked to the unit listings. A word after the price, such as *cash* or *instalment*, shows under it. With no table, the unit listings (Multi Units) are used. |
| Amenities | Up to six feature tiles for the stand-out amenities (pool, sky lounge, gym, cinema and so on), each with an icon and a one-line description, then a checklist of everything else. The items come from bold-titled groups in the description's *Amenities* section, otherwise the *Features & Amenities* ticks. |

**Look and feel.** The page uses block.ke's warm off-white with sand-coloured sections. Navy is used for the key facts strip, the hero enquiry card, Amenities and *Why Block*, with brass accents. Headings are set in Instrument Serif, a Google Font, and a few words in each are picked out in brass italics, for example "What comes *with the keys.*". To accent part of the overview headline override, wrap those words in asterisks: `About *the building*`. Setting `display_font` to `''` returns all headings to the site font.

**Amenity tiles.** Tiles are chosen by kind, best first, and appear in full rows of 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6. The kinds are: beach, pool, sky lounge or sky garden, cinema, spa, gym, nature, kids' play, café, golf, gardens, yoga, shops, co-working, sports courts, BBQ, residents' lounge and jogging track. A tile keeps the listing's own name when it is short. A longer item becomes the tile's description, under its own name when it starts with one ("Rooftop terrace" over "Rooftop terrace with jacuzzi area"), else under a short title ("Sky garden" over "Terrace sky garden with panoramic city views"). Security, parking, lifts, power and water go in the checklist, titled "The everyday essentials" when that is all it holds.

**Amenity photos.** Give a listing's photos a caption in the Media Library (*Media → the photo → Caption*), for example "Indoor heated pool", "Sky Lounge" or "Private cinema". Photos whose caption names an amenity then appear as a captioned photo strip at the top of Amenities, before the tiles. On phones it swipes; on desktop it shows one full row of 2, 3 or 4 photos (6 or 8 in two rows). Tapping a photo opens the gallery on it. Other captions, such as "Lantana Road frontage", show on the gallery photos and in the photo viewer. With fewer than two amenity captions, the section looks as before. The caption is WordPress's own field, so the theme may also show it wherever it displays captions.

**Turn it on for one listing.** Edit the property, find the **Development page** box, and set **Layout: On**. The box also has optional overrides: hero line, key facts, deposit, reservation fee, payment plan text, calculator price, places, photo labels, brochure PDF, video and similar listings.

**Photo labels (v1.3.2).** Off-plan pages label the main photo "Project render" and the gallery "Project renders.". For show-house or site photos, set **Main photo label** (e.g. *Show apartment*) and **Gallery note** (e.g. *Photos of the show apartment.*) in the box, or the custom fields `bke_dev_hero_label` and `bke_dev_gallery_note`. "none" hides either.

**Preview before switching on.** While logged in, add `?bke_layout=1` to any property URL, or use the admin-bar menu *Development layout → Preview*. Use `?bke_layout=0` to see the classic page.

**Turn it on for every development at once.** Set the option `blockke_dev_page['auto'] = true`. Every listing with units (`property_has_subunits = 1`) then uses the layout unless its box says *Off*.

**Land estates (v1.6.1).** A plot estate such as Zuri Springs gets the same layout in land words. A listing counts as land when it has a land category (Land, Residential land, Agricultural, Industrial land), no home category (Apartments, Villas, Houses, Studio) and no bedrooms; a plot listed under a land estate counts too. Set **Listing kind** in the box (`bke_dev_kind`: `land` or `homes`) to override that, for example on an estate that sells both plots and built homes. On a land page:

- the hero reads "Plots for sale" with *From*, *Plots from* (the smallest plot) and the deposit, and its form offers the price list and site plan;
- the key facts come from the *at a glance* rows: estate size, land use, servicing, tenure, payment plan and distance from Nairobi;
- a *Plot type | Size | Price from* table becomes *Plot sizes and prices*, filtered by plot type, with each row's servicing kept in the enquiry ("Residential, unserviced · 1/8 acre");
- *Infrastructure and amenities* puts roads, power and water first; items under a heading that names one part of the estate ("Lulu Park serviced plots") go in the checklist with that heading, never as estate-wide tiles; a power line or wayleave is left out; a paragraph after the last list becomes the line under *Also listed*;
- the payment section shows the developer's plan and the instalment calculator only when the listing states a deposit or a term (*Instalment period* in the box, `bke_dev_plan_months`, or "over up to three years" in the text); otherwise *Ask for the payment plan* and what a plot costs on top of its price. Never a mortgage calculator;
- *Also consider* shows other land for sale in the area and its neighbours (config `land_neighbours`), with "See all land for sale in Juja";
- *Why Block*, the disclaimer, the forms (*Build / Invest / Both*), the pop-ups and the lead email ("Type: Land (plots)") use land wording, and the search button links to land in the area.

The landing page (`?lp=1`) of a land listing for sale uses the same words. Switching `land_layout` off returns every page to the v1.6 wording.

**Construction progress (v1.7).** A development page can show what is happening on site, for buyers who cannot visit. Everything shown comes from a dated site report that the team has entered: the developer's photos and notes for each part of the site, and the report PDF.

- **Where it shows.** Under the photos, before Residences: "On site", *Construction progress* (*Work on site* on land pages). The latest report is shown as cards: each card has a photo, the part of the site and what was done. Tap a photo to open all the report's photos in the viewer, each captioned "Block D1 — 15 October 2026". On phones the viewer uses the full screen width, with the arrows over the photo's edges. Older reports sit under *Earlier updates*, closed. A *Site update · 15 October 2026* link appears under the hero facts, and *Progress* appears in the section bar. The hero link is not shown on the landing page's phone cover, where it would push the form off the first screen. On phones, a report with more than four parts shows the first three and a *Show all 8 parts* button; without JavaScript, all parts show.
- **Who says so.** The section names the source ("Tatu City Limited's site report of 15 October 2026") and adds: "Photos and notes are the developer's; Block has not inspected the site."
- **Freshness.** A report older than 120 days loses the hero link. The section then reads *Last report* instead of *Updated*, its button reads *Ask for a newer site report*, and the line beside it reads "We'll ask the developer for their latest report and send it, with photos, to your WhatsApp."
- **No report yet.** An off-plan page shows a short panel instead: its completion date and *Ask for site photos*. Ready homes and land show nothing.
- **Enquiries.** *Get the next site update on WhatsApp* (under a current report) opens the pop-up headed *The next site update*; the lead email's form line reads *Site progress request (next report)*, and the buyer is waiting for the developer's next report. *Ask for a newer site report* and *Ask for site photos* open *Site photos and progress*; the form line reads *Site progress request*, and the buyer wants the latest photos now.

**Add a report.**

1. Upload the photos and the PDF in **Media → Add New**, not from the listing's edit screen. A PDF attached to the listing becomes its brochure when the *Brochure* field is empty.
2. Note each file's ID: the number after `post=` in the address when you open the file.
3. Edit the property. In the **Development page** box, write the report in **Site progress** (custom field `bke_dev_progress`). Only administrators see this field.

Write one report per block of lines. A blank line ends a report, so leave none inside one:

- First line: the date, then `|` who reported it, then `|` `PDF` and a Media ID or URL. The last two are optional and can come in either order.
- Then one line per part of the site: `name | what was done | photo IDs or URLs`, with the photos separated by commas (or semicolons) and optional.
- A line with no `|` is a note for that report (two at most).
- Lines starting with `#` are ignored.

```
15 October 2026 | Tatu City Limited | PDF 45120
Block D1 | First-floor slab (parking) and ground-floor slab (apartments) cast; ready for the aluminium formwork. | 45101
Block A1 | Ground-floor slab cast; waterproofing of the retaining walls in progress. | 45102
Show house | Façade about 95% done; landscaping almost done. | 45110, 45111, 45112
```

- **Dates** can be written "15 October 2026", "15 Oct 2026", "October 15, 2026", "2026-10-15", "15/10/2026", "15.10.2026" or "15-10-2026" (day first), or "October 2026". Day first, a two-digit year is read as 20xx: "15/10/26", "15.10.26", "15-10-26", "15 Oct 26" or "15-Oct-26". A label before the date ("Report:", "Update:", "Site report:", "Date:") and "of" after the day ("15th of October 2026") are ignored. Reports can be in any order; the newest is shown first. A date line the page cannot read (a typo such as "15 Septmber 2026", or "10/15/2026") is left out with all its lines up to the next date line. So is a block after a blank line that does not start with a date ("Week 38, 2026", "September 2026 report"). Nothing is ever shown under another report's date.
- **Limits.** At most 12 reports, 24 parts per report and 6 photos per part. A name is cut at 60 characters, a description at 400 and a note at 300.
- **What is left out.** Photos must be images in this site's Media Library. Files from other sites, private or binned media, unknown IDs and dates still ahead are left out. A bare number in the second cell ("Block D1 | 40") is the description, never a photo. Anything the page cannot read is skipped and never breaks the page.
- **Check after saving.** Under the field, the edit screen says what the page shows (the latest report's date, parts, photos and PDF) and lists, by line number, every line, photo or PDF it could not read.
- **Units.** A unit listing with an empty field shows its development's reports.

**Reading the listing more carefully (v1.8).** Development pages now read the listing more carefully. These fixes come from the villa assessments. Classic pages, the classic calculator, area, index and search pages and lead emails are unchanged.

- **Names.** The page heading is the development's name rather than a selling phrase from the title, whenever the listing gives one. "3 Bedroom Villa for Sale, Tilisi Views Limuru" becomes "Tilisi Views". The name comes from the custom field `bke_dev_name`, then, for a unit, its development page's `bke_dev_name`, then the Project term. The Project term is written the way the listing's title (or its development's) writes it, without a stray full stop: "17 zuri apartments" reads "17 Zuri Apartments", and "Kitusuru Grove ." reads "Kitisuru Grove" when the title says "The Kitisuru Grove". A term in lower case that no title spells better gets capitals on its words. Renaming badly cased terms is still the better fix. A trailing area such as ", Kiambu Road" is dropped, since the area label above already shows it. `bke_dev_name` now also names the breadcrumb, the section bar, the forms and the photo note.
- **Hero and key facts.**
  - A villa or house at one fixed price reads *Price*, off-plan too. It keeps *From* when its text gives that price as a starting price ("from KES …", "Starting price: KES …", "KES … onwards", "and above") or a range, or when its title offers more than one bedroom count. Off-plan apartments, studios and offices keep *From*: their price usually starts a range of floors and views.
  - The completion cell shows a short month ("Dec 2026", "Before Dec 2026"), and a long area such as "Ongata Rongai" fits on phones.
  - Sizes are the smallest and largest area stated, read from "Built-up area" or "Plinth area" and never from a plot, site or court. A terrace, balcony, garden or plot figure in the same value is not the home's size. A single home with no size in its text uses its size field, unless its title offers a range of homes ("2–4 Bed Lofts") or it has no bedrooms and isn't a studio. Square feet become whole square metres.
  - A completion row counts only when it gives a date: "Completion status | Complete" is a status. A ready home whose only completion row says "To be confirmed at point of enquiry" reads *Completion: On request*, not *Status: Ready*, unless its status terms say it is complete or ready now.
- **Payment.**
  - Instalments are offered only when the listing offers them, not when it lists them as still to be confirmed or as not applicable.
  - The calculator runs over the plan's stated length ("over 24 months", with a choice when several lengths are stated). When the text runs the plan to completion ("over 20 months, running through to the Q4 2027 completion date", "phased over the construction period"), the calculator runs up to completion instead. A stated length that runs past the completion date is used, and the note gives the completion date. "Up to 9 months" is noted as the longest the listing states. **Instalment period** now works for homes as well.
  - It takes no share on completion from the cash, mortgage or bank-loan route.
  - A payment table's header row is no longer shown as a step.
- **Who it suits and investors.** Long bullets are kept. "It may be less suitable for …" is read as the note on who it suits less well. The investor view leaves out lead-ins to lists it does not show. It adds a caveat that comes after its third sentence ("not guaranteed", "should not be assumed", "depends on …"), never a claim such as "Guaranteed 10% rental yield".
- **Amenities.**
  - "Coffee" alone is not a café, "dining" is not a residents' lounge, and "recycled water" is not a jogging track. Yoga comes before lawns, and pickleball counts as a sports court.
  - A long item keeps its own name as the tile title, for example "Rooftop terrace" over "Rooftop terrace with jacuzzi area".
  - The tile lines make no claims a home may not bear out: no "above the city", "steps from home" or "no membership".
  - The checklist is titled "The everyday essentials" only when it holds nothing but practical items such as security, parking, lifts, power, water, the kitchen and laundry. A fireplace or a bar makes it "Also included".
- **Residences.**
  - A row links to the unit whose own words it carries: "Premium" for the Premium townhouse, "Village 1" for Village 1. Every 4-bed row no longer links to the one 4-bed unit. A "Studio" row links to the only studio unit, and "+ DSQ" or "all en-suite" does not set a type apart.
  - "3 & 4 Bedroom" reads "3 – 4 Bed".
- **Location.**
  - A *Destination | Drive time* table is read.
  - "~15 minutes | to Westlands" is turned round to "Westlands | ~15 minutes".
  - Bold topics and sentences are not listed as places, unless they name places with a time ("Schools | Cavina School and Kenton College, about 5 to 10 minutes").
  - The note says "travel times" only when a place has one, in its value or its name.
- **Landing pages.**
  - `?lp=1` also works for a listing with no Buy/Rent term whose title does not say rent.
  - On the phone cover, the area is not repeated. Without a residences table, the cover shows the bedrooms the title names: "Studio", "3 Bed", and nothing for a range such as "4 & 5 Bedroom Homes". With no count in the title, it shows the text's own Bedrooms row, or a villa's or house's bedrooms.
  - The map search no longer repeats the area.
- **Copy.** The *Also consider* heading names the bedrooms that its "See all" link names. Houses are "built", not "while the building goes up". Phrases like "Late 2027" are lower-case mid-sentence ("until completion in late 2027").

**Payment plans, floor plans and tile names (v1.8.1).** These fixes come from the Super West View pages. Classic pages, land pages, and area, index and search pages are unchanged.

- **Payment.** An off-plan page shows the instalment calculator only when the listing states instalments: it offers them, gives the plan's length or a share every month or quarter, says the balance is paid monthly, in stages, during construction or until completion, offers a flexible plan, or pays a cash route's balance over time. *Instalment period* or *Instalments until* in the box state them too. A listing that states a deposit or a reservation fee but no instalments, such as Super West View's "20% deposit, balance under the developer's payment plan", is headed "20% deposit, then the developer's payment plan", keeps its own steps, and shows an *Ask for the payment plan* panel with what the listing states (our own sums are marked as ours) and the *Get the official payment plan* button. Fees, rent, service charges and checklists never count as instalments. A listing that states nothing at all keeps *Ask for the payment plan* with the estimate, as in v1.4.5.
- **Floor plans.** The forms offer floor plans only when the listing has them: its own text offers them ("Floor plans are available on request"; not "not yet available" or an "open floor plan"), it links or embeds a brochure or floor-plan PDF, or it has WPResidence floor plans with an image, a brochure, an attached PDF that is not a site report, or an image named as a floor plan. A unit also uses its development's, and a development its units'. Otherwise the hero form reads *Get the price list*, the residences rows *Price & availability*, the pop-up, contact box and WhatsApp message name the price list only, and that lead's email (and Zoho's Description) says "Form: Price and availability request". The form sends the same fields. To offer floor plans on a listing whose text doesn't mention them, add a line such as "Floor plans are available on request" to its description.
- **Floor plans by default (v1.8.2).** Block has the floor plans, so every development page that isn't land offers them again, as in v1.8: *Get the price list and floor plans*, *Floor plan & price* in the residences rows, and "Form: Floor plan & price request" in the lead email. Land pages keep the site plan. To go back to the per-listing check above, set `'floor_plans_default' => false` in the config.
- **Amenity tiles.** "Residents" on its own no longer makes a residents' lounge tile ("Ample residents' parking" goes in the checklist); a lounge, club, clubhouse, common room or the development's own community hall still does, but a nearby community centre doesn't. A tile's own name stops at the first sentence end ("Swimming pool. Shared with the building's other residents." is titled "Swimming pool"; not after "Mon." or "Blk."), and a name that would end in "a", "an", "the", "and" or an -ly adverb gives way to the earlier drawing.

**Homes for rent (v1.9.1, every part switched off until its switch is on).** A home for rent can read as a rental, on the development layout and on its classic page. Every part sits behind its own switch in the option `blockke_dev_page` (see *Settings*), and every switch is off when v1.9.1 is installed, so pages and leads stay as they were until a switch is written. The one exception is `lp_rent_guard`, on from the start: a rental never gets an ad landing page (section 4). An editor can look at each switch before it is on, by adding its preview parameter to the address: `?bke_rent=1`, `?bke_rcards=1`, `?bke_cls=1`, `?bke_forms=1`, `?bke_gal=1` and `?bke_ld=1`. (v1.9.1 is the release name of the rental work; its comments in the file say v1.8.3.)

A listing counts as a home for rent when its Buy/Rent term is *For Rent* without *For Sale*, or, with no Buy/Rent term, when its title says "for rent" or "to let" (with `rent_pages` on, also "to rent" or "for lease", never "Rent-to-Own") and does not offer both: "for Rent & Sale" keeps the sale page, as a listing tagged both does. Land for lease keeps its own words (land mode). Sale pages, land pages, and area, index and search pages are unchanged by `rent_pages`.

With **`rent_pages`** on, a home for rent's development page reads as a rental:

- **Hero.** The status line reads "For rent", not "Ready to move in · For rent". The facts read *Rent / month* with the exact rent (KES 200,000), then the availability the listing states: its *Availability* row ("Available"), else its *Viewing* row ("By appointment"), else *Availability: On request*. They never read *Price*, *Status: Ready*, *Completion* or *Deposit*.
- **Renting.** A short *Renting* section takes the place of the payment plan or mortgage, and the section bar links to it as *Renting*. It shows the rent ("KES 200,000 a month"); what the listing states about the deposit, lease, service charge, furnishing, availability and viewing, read from its glance or snapshot rows and its *Rental Terms*, *Lease Terms* or *Payment Plan* section (a value still to be confirmed, such as "confirmed at point of enquiry", is not shown; a deposit or lease needs a figure, and a service charge an amount or "included"); a line naming what is still confirmed at enquiry; and a *See it in person* panel with the *Arrange a viewing* button. There is no calculator, deposit estimate, financing button or investor view.
- **Forms.** They all ask about availability and a viewing, never the price list, floor plans or payment plan: the hero form (*Arrange a viewing*, button *Check availability*), the section bar and mobile bar (*Arrange a viewing*), the pop-up ("Availability and viewing times, sent to your WhatsApp."), the enquiry section, and the thank-you line ("… to arrange a viewing of …"). The full form and the follow-up questions ask "When would you like to move in?" (This month, 1–3 months, Later) and drop *Buying to*.
- **WhatsApp.** "Hi Block, I'm interested in renting Runda Paradise 1 (KES 200,000 a month). Is it still available, and when could I arrange a viewing? Ref W-43543." Every WhatsApp link on a rent page, the ones its script builds included, says "viewing", carries the *Ref* and is marked `data-blkct="1"`, so the site-wide snippet 40769 (contact click tracking and WhatsApp prefill) leaves its text alone. The name drops "to Let", "for Rent", "to Rent" and "for Lease".
- **Lead emails and Zoho.** The email reads "RENTAL ENQUIRY (property page)", "Rental: Runda Paradise 1 - KES 200,000 a month" and "Move-in: This month", with the subject "[Block] Rental enquiry: {name} - {listing}". The listing goes by the page's name (*Development name*, else the Project term or the name in the title), not a whole SEO title. The form lines are "Hero form (viewing)", "Viewing request", "Availability and viewing request" (a residences row) and "Comparable rents request". The follow-up answers arrive as "MORE DETAILS ON A RENTAL ENQUIRY". Zoho gets the same description, Company "Individual tenant" instead of "Individual buyer", and "Rental" in the owner's *Enquiry type* field once `zoho_enquiry_field` names it. The saved lead carries `kind` rent (see *Enquiries*).
- **The rest of the page.** *Why Block* and the disclaimer are a tenant's (settings `rent_why`, `rent_disclaimer`). The FAQ heading is "Questions tenants ask", and the checklist note says "tenants". Similar rentals carry no *Ready now* badge, and the key facts have no completion. There is still no `?lp=1` landing page for a rental.
- **What the rent is per, and its currency.** One phrase is used everywhere (page, WhatsApp, lead, cards, structured data): "a month" when the after-price label says month or is empty, or the price is per sq ft ("KES 130 per sq ft a month"); otherwise "a week", "a day", "a night" or "a year" ("KES 6,500 a day", hero *Rent / day*). A label such as "Rent on application", no price, or a rent over KES 2M with no label reads *Rent on request*. A rent stated in US dollars (the custom field `bke_rent_usd`, else a glance or rental-terms row such as "Rent | USD 5,500 a month") reads "USD 5,500 a month" with "The rent is set in US dollars." **Segment pages**, 20 of them, each holding one kind and Buy or Rent:
- homes: `homes-`, `apartments-`, `villas-`, `houses-` and `studios-for-sale` / `-for-rent`;
- commercial: `commercial-`, `offices-`, `warehouses-` and `shops-for-sale` / `-for-rent`;
- land: `land-for-sale`, `land-for-rent` ("Land for lease").

**What goes in a grid.** Sold and let listings are left out. A development is one card (its main listing, else its cheapest matching home; two listings of the same Project share it), priced *From* its cheapest matching home, with its bedroom range and how many homes. A listing for several homes reads its bedrooms from the title ("Studio, 1-Bed & 2-Bed" → *Studio–2 bed*) and shows no single size. Kind comes from the category: land categories are land; commercial, offices, warehouses and shops are commercial unless the listing is also a home with bedrooms (a flat above shops stays a home); villas and houses by their category; studios by the Studio category. A per-sq-ft price is shown as such, sorts last, and is left out of the "from … to …" range (a commercial segment that mixes the two shows no range).

**Search engines.** A segment page has its own title ("Villas for Sale in Lavington, Nairobi | Block Real Estate", dropping ", Nairobi" past 60 characters), description ("Homes for sale in Westlands, Nairobi: 28 listings from KES 4.2M to KES 23.5M. …"), canonical and Open Graph tags, set through Rank Math's filters (and printed directly when Rank Math is off). Rank Math's rel next/prev is switched off on these pages. **On phones** the hero form and key facts are left out so the listings come straight after the heading (on a segment page the first card starts on the first screen), each section ends with its own "See all" link, and the bottom bar ("Ask an advisor", WhatsApp) appears once past the hero. Commercial and land pages say "property" or "land" rather than "homes", link "Search" to the site search instead of the homes search sheet, and their forms ask no bedrooms.

**Enquiries** go through the same endpoint with no listing, the area and the segment. The email reads "[Block] Area enquiry: Jane - Villas for sale in Lavington", with what they are looking for (Buy or Rent, type, bedrooms, budget). Zoho gets the same text in the description; its budget field is left empty, as its picklist uses the price-check popup's bands. **Turning it on.** Off by default. Logged-in editors can preview any area with `?bke_area=1` (and `?bke_area=0` for the classic page), also from the admin bar's "Area page" menu. Then set `area_pages` in the option to a list of area slugs, e.g. `array( 'lavington', 'westlands' )`, or `true` for all. The segment URLs need WordPress's rewrite rules refreshed; the snippet does it by itself whenever its rule is missing from the stored rules (first run, or after the rules were rebuilt while the snippet was off). After switching the snippet off for good, save **Settings → Permalinks** once so the segment URLs stop resolving. Each area's data is cached (`bkd_area_{term id}`) and rebuilt whenever a listing changes, as similar homes are.

**Tracking.** `area_filter`, `area_sort`, `area_more`, `area_card_click` (with position and section), plus the usual enquiry events with `area_page` set to the segment.

## 7. Search results (v1.6, switched off)

A listing for several sizes ("Studio, 1-Bed & 2-Bed Apartments") stands for its development and is never listed as one home, and the card's bedrooms include the sizes in such titles. A development's only listing in a section (a single home, or one flat to rent in a building selling off-plan) is set like every other card, so a row never mixes two kinds: its building's name, then "Riverside · 3 bed · 160 m²", then "KES 12.5M · Completion 2027", and it opens that home. With one area searched, place lines leave the area out, as on the area pages. With a price filter the card shows the range of the homes that match ("KES 8M–22.5M"). A listing with no photo at all is a line under "Also listed (3)" at the foot of its section, never a placeholder tile. "New on Block" goes on at most 1 card in 5. 18 cards per section (`index_page_size`), then "Show 18 more": a link to `?n=36` that, with JavaScript, adds the next cards in place and keeps `n` in the address, so a reload or Back shows the same list.

**Matching.** Buy or Rent and kind follow the area pages' rules. Bedrooms follow `beds_mode` (4 means four and more). A price band takes homes priced inside it; a development's main listing matches when its from–to range overlaps the band; per-sq-ft prices are left out while a price is set. Completion uses the listing's Completion term, else its development's stated date; Ready now takes completed buildings. Recommended order is the theme's: featured, then newest. Also "Price, lowest first", "Price, highest first" and "Newest on Block".

**The bar** is an ordinary GET form (Buy or rent, Area, Bedrooms for homes, Price with sale or rent bands, Completion for homes for sale, Sort), so it works without JavaScript ("Show results"). With JavaScript, changes apply at once from 1000px; on phones and tablets the bar is one row, "Filters · 2" and Sort, as on the new developments page, and "Filters" opens the same full-screen sheet as the new developments and segment pages: "Filters" with a close button, each field labelled above a boxed select, and a button that counts as the visitor chooses ("Show 12 homes", "Show 5 listings" when other kinds are in the results), with "Clear filters" beside it when a filter is set (Escape or the close button returns). The count is asked of the results page itself (`?bke_count=1`, a small JSON answer, only while the results are ours). A change made from the keyboard (arrow keys step through a closed select) waits for Enter or for the select to lose focus, so each key press doesn't reload the page; a choice made from the opened list (Space, F4 or Alt+Down, then Enter) applies at once, like a click. Set filters, and a sort other than Recommended, carry a 2px brass underline; bar selects are 15px, as on the other pages. It submits only our own clean parameters, so the theme's are translated on the first change, and it carries `data-bke-own` and always a `completion` field so 41011's form shim adds nothing to it. Areas offered: those with at least 3 developments, busiest first, then "Other areas".

**No or few results.** "No 4+ bed homes for sale in Riverside completing in 2026." then up to three wider searches with their counts, most results first ("Any area (16)", "Lavington (5)", "Kilimani (4)"), nearby areas from `neighbours`, and "Send this search to an advisor" (WhatsApp, pre-filled: "Hi Block, I'm looking for homes for sale: Riverside, 4+ bed, completing 2026. Please send current options and price lists." and the link; prices and dates in the new developments page's words, "under KES 8M", "completing 2029 and later", "ready now"). With one or two results the same links follow them under "Close matches". Every results page ends with the WhatsApp link and the area disclaimer.

**Redirects** (`search_redirect`, off; 302 only, not cached, campaign tags kept). One action, one area whose area page is on, one type (bedrooms or a completion alone mean homes) and a price only if it is exactly a band: to that area's segment page, e.g. `/property_area/westlands/apartments-for-sale/`, with `beds`, `price`, `completion` and `sort`. Until `card_v16` is on, segment pages don't read those, so a search that uses them stays on the results page instead. An empty segment page is never a destination, and a search with no type (a header search for any kind, or "Search property" on a commercial page) stays on the results, which show every kind. Buying anywhere with a completion year, when the new developments index is on (`dev_index`): to `/new-developments/?completion=2027` with bedrooms and price. `?bke_search` stops the redirect for an editor.

**Search engines.** Always `noindex, follow`, canonical `/advanced-search/`, Rank Math's rel next/prev off, the title from the search ("2 bed apartments for sale in Westlands | Block Real Estate"). "Show more" links are `nofollow`.

**Tracking.** `search_view` (`count_devs`, `count_homes`, `segments`), `search_card_click` (`target_id`, `position`, `kind`: group, unit, dev or text, `segment`, `vt`), `search_match_click` (`target_id`, `unit_id`, `row`, or `all` for the price list link), `index_no_results` (once per search, not again on a reload or Back) and `index_relax` (`filters` / `removed`), and the existing `click_whatsapp`.

**Settings.** `search_page` (9), `search_layout`, `search_redirect`, `search_params`, `search_ignore`, `search_map_page` (39161), `index_page_size` (18), `budget_bands`, `beds_mode`, `neighbours`, `area_disclaimer`. Before switching it on, submit the home page search on block.ke (Buy, Westlands, 2 bed, a price) and check that the admin bar says the search can use the new layout; any key it names goes into `search_ignore` (ignored) or `search_params` (read).

**Tested** on the local copy with block.ke's 318 developments and about 850 listings, with snippet 41011 running: 162 checks at 390, 768 and 1366px, with and without JavaScript. ## 8. From a card to a development and back (v1.6, switched off)

What happens when a visitor opens a development from a v1.6 card (the new developments index, search results, and area and segment pages once they use the new cards) and comes back.

**The development remembers the list.** A plain click on a card (not a new tab) keeps a note for that browser tab: which card, the list's address and name, and the bedrooms the visitor was looking at. Card links stay clean permalinks. For the next 30 minutes, on that development:
- the residences filter starts on the visitor's bedrooms, with "Showing 2 bed homes only. Show all" above the table ("Showing studios only", and "… from your search." when the card was on search results), only when the table has that filter. As on the lists, 4 means four and more, so a table that also has 5 bed homes stays on All without a note;
- the link above the name reads "Back to new developments" (or "Back to Westlands", "Back to homes for sale in Westlands", "Back to your search"), and still steps back in the browser's history, so the list comes back exactly as it was. It is 44px to tap and always one line: on phones Search and Share become round icons beside it (a very long label ends in "…"); from 700px it heads the breadcrumb on a line of its own; from 1000px the way back, the breadcrumb and Search and Share share one bar across the top of the hero, above the text and the photo, so the breadcrumb no longer stacks up in the narrow text column. Without a note the existing "Back to results" rules apply.

A note is ignored when the visitor reached the development again from another page.

**Coming back.** When the browser keeps the list in its back/forward cache, the list returns exactly as it was. When it doesn't, the list scrolls the card to the middle of the screen and puts the keyboard focus on it (the focus ring shows only to visitors who opened the card from the keyboard); if the card is gone, it returns to where the visitor was. The browser's own scroll restoration is paused only for that return. The way back is kept in the list's own history entry, so it still works after the visitor has opened cards on other lists in between. With `view_transitions` on, Chrome and Edge hold the list's first paint until that card has loaded and centre it before showing the page, so even a slow phone opens the list at the card rather than at the top.

**The photo transition (`view_transitions`).** In Chrome and Edge, the card's photo grows into the development's main photo and shrinks back on the way back; the rest of the page cross-fades. Only the photo moves. It is skipped with *Reduce motion*, *Data Saver*, on 2G and on phones with 2 GB of memory or less; other browsers load the page as before. With the switch on, development pages show the main photo first, at 3:2 like the cards: full width at the top on phones and tablets, 7 of 12 columns beside the name and form from 1000px with photos 2 and 3 under it, two across (each opens the viewer there), held in view while the text beside it scrolls. The "Project render" label moves from the photo to the gallery viewer (the disclaimer already mentions renders). Ad landing pages are never changed.

**Prefetch (`prefetch`).** When the pointer rests on a card (or a finger touches it), Chrome fetches the development page in advance, so it opens at once. Never on *Data Saver* or 2G, and nothing is prerendered, so analytics run once. A fetch is not counted as a view; when the visitor opens the fetched page, the page counts it then (`POST /wp-json/block/v1/view`, which exists only while `prefetch` is on, counts published development pages, not editors' own visits, and at most once per visitor address and development every 10 minutes). The visitor's address is Cloudflare's `CF-Connecting-IP` only when the request really came from one of Cloudflare's published addresses (listed in `blockke_bkn_is_cf()`), otherwise the connection's own, so a request sent straight to the server can't make up a new address each time. Known limit, accepted: visitors who share one public address, as many do on Kenyan mobile networks, count once per development every 10 minutes when they open it from a prefetched copy; direct visits and reloads count every time as before.

**Turning it on.** Both switches are off. Editors can preview the photo-first hero on any development page with `?bke_vt=1` (and `?bke_vt=0`); the transition itself needs the switch, as the parameter is not carried from page to page. Logged-in editors always get the list note on development pages, with the way back and the hero's top bar as described above, so previews of the new lists work end to end. With every v1.6 switch off, development and landing pages are byte for byte as in v1.5 for visitors.

**Tracking.** `context_beds` (bedrooms preset from the card; once, not again on a reload, Back or Forward) and `back_to_results` with `source` (index, area, segment, search or other).

## Enquiries

Every form posts to `/wp-json/block/v1/development-lead`, which:

- saves the enquiry in the option `blockke_dev_leads` (the last 200) before Zoho and the email, so a slow or failing step can't lose it;
- emails the enquiry addresses (`notify_emails`; on block.ke loyd@block.ke, sales@block.ke and loydmokaya@gmail.com) and the listing's agent;
- forwards the lead to Zoho through the popup snippet's Web-to-Lead connection, once its tokens are set;
- tags the source (Google Ads, Meta, organic and so on) using the same rules as the popup;
- fires the Google Ads lead conversion (`window.blockkeLeadConversion`), GA4 `generate_lead` and Meta `Lead`.

If the listing has a brochure, either a PDF link in the box or a PDF attached to the listing, it opens straight after the visitor sends their details.

**Optional questions after a quick enquiry.** The hero and pop-up forms ask only for a name and phone number. Once the lead is in, the thank-you panel offers three one-tap questions: which home, when they would like to buy (within 3 months, 3–12 months, just exploring) and whether they are buying to live in or invest. Answers are added to the same lead in `blockke_dev_leads` and emailed as "[Block] More details: …"; add them to the Zoho lead by hand, as Web-to-Lead can't update a lead. Skipping them loses nothing. The full enquiry form asks the timeline as well. The questions are not a separate form, so GA4 and Meta form tracking don't count the lead twice.

On a home for rent (with `rent_pages` on) the question is when they would like to move in (this month, 1–3 months, later), with no "buying to" question, and the email reads "Move-in" (v1.9.1).

**Rental enquiries (v1.9.1).** With `rent_pages` on, a lead from a home for rent's development page or classic page arrives as "[Block] Rental enquiry: {name} - {listing}", with "RENTAL ENQUIRY (property page)" or "RENTAL ENQUIRY (classic page)", "Rental: {name} - KES 200,000 a month" and "Move-in" in the email and in Zoho's Description. An area or segment page lead whose *Looking to* is *Rent* arrives as "[Block] Rental enquiry: {name} - Homes for rent in Kileleshwa" (its description stays "AREA ENQUIRY (area page)"). Only the full area form (`blockke_area_full_form()`) has *Looking to*; the v1.6 area and segment pages that are live use `blockke_area_v16_form()`, which sends none, so their rental leads keep today's area subject until that form gains the field. An area lead's follow-up answers also still arrive as "MORE DETAILS ON A DEVELOPMENT ENQUIRY", since it names no listing. These leads are saved in `blockke_dev_leads` with `'kind' => 'rent'` (sale leads have no `kind`), post Company "Individual tenant" to Zoho where the popup's map sends "Individual buyer", and send "Rental" in the owner's Zoho *Enquiry type* field once `zoho_enquiry_field` names it (sale leads never send it, so Zoho's default applies: set it to "Sale"). The enquiries inbox v1.1 shows each lead's Type (Sale, Rental, Area, Index, Topic, Consultation). An editor's preview lead (`?bke_rent=1` while `rent_pages` is off) is a rental lead too. Before switching `rent_pages` on, add a Zoho Mail filter for "[Block] Rental enquiry" and check any rules keyed on "[Block] Development enquiry" or "DEVELOPMENT ENQUIRY".

**Spam and AutoFill.** Each form has a hidden field that people never see and bots fill in. A send that fills it is answered as if it worked, but nothing is emailed or saved as a lead; the last 30 are kept in `blockke_dev_spam` in case one was a person. Before v1.3.1 the field was called `company`, which browsers' AutoFill fills in, so enquiries from visitors using AutoFill were dropped without a trace. It now has a name AutoFill leaves alone and isn't drawn at all. When a send fails, the form shows the error code (for example "error 403") before the WhatsApp link, so the cause can be traced.

**Pages built outside the snippet (lead topics).** A page that is not a listing, an area page or the new developments page, such as the land-and-build page, can post to the same endpoint with a `topic` instead of a listing. The topic must be a key of `lead_topics` in the settings (by default `'land-build' => 'Land and build'`; setting `lead_topics` in the option `blockke_dev_page` replaces that list, so keep `land-build` in it). The lead is saved in `blockke_dev_leads` (the inbox shows "Development page (land-build page: …)"), sent to Zoho with `where` as its area, and emailed as "[Block] Land and build enquiry: Jane - A plot and a house", with any `stage`, `where` and `budget` lines the page sends. The follow-up answers work as on other pages, and there is no brochure. A lead with an unknown topic or none still gets "This listing is no longer available.", and a topic sent with a listing, an area or `index` is ignored, so those leads are unchanged. The page should not use the development pages' form names (`hero`, `modal`, `contact`, `progress` and so on) as its `source` or `intent`, because those print the development page's form labels.

## Getting around: search, similar homes and the unit switcher (v1.4)

**Search homes.** A "Search" button sits next to Share at the top of every development page, and a search icon sits in the desktop section bar. It opens a short search already filled in from the listing, for example Buy · Westlands · 2 bed · KES 12M–18M. "Show homes" opens the site's own search results (`/advanced-search/?location=Westlands&property_action_category[]=for-sale&componentsbeds=2&price_low=…&price_max=…`, plus `completion` for sales). "Or ask an advisor to shortlist for me" turns the same search into an enquiry. On phones the browser's back button closes the search. Without JavaScript the button is a plain link to the filled-in search.

**Studios (v1.4.2).** Bedrooms in the search start at *Studio*. Because the site search does not filter on 0 bedrooms, picking Studio searches the site's Studio category instead (`property_category[]=studio-apartments`, set by `studio_cat`; `''` removes the option). Studio pages link the same way: "See all studio homes in Westlands" under Also consider, and the Studio chip in a development's Residences. Studios are stored as 0 bedrooms, and a studio needs the Studio category to appear in these results.

**Also consider.** On a home's page (a unit or a single listing), this shows up to 6 homes with the same number of bedrooms. It picks them in this order:
1. Homes in the same area within 25% of the price, closest in price and size first.
2. Homes in the same area at any price.
3. Homes in the nearest neighbouring areas (config `neighbours`).
4. Homes with one bedroom more or fewer in the area.
5. The next ring of areas, only while fewer than 3 have been found.

It shows one home per development, and never this development or sold, let, commercial, office or per-sq-ft-priced listings. A villa or house (v1.4.4) is compared only with villas and houses, and when nothing turns up nearby, the developments shown are the villas and houses closest in price rather than the newest developments. When nothing matches, the message names the development its own homes were left out of ("No other 3-bedroom homes in Limuru outside Tilisi Views listed right now"). Rentals are compared with rentals. A studio stored as a 1-bedroom is recognised from its title, or from a size under 500 sq ft when the title gives no bedroom count (so a compact "1 Bedroom" stays a 1-bed). Listings for a range of homes ("2–4 Bed Lofts", "Studio & 1 Bed") are left out. Under the cards are "See all 2-bed homes in Westlands" (the site search), "Refine search" and "Compare these with an advisor". Development pages show similar developments instead. If fewer than 3 matches turn up, the block shows developments in the area. If those are short too, it shows the one or two homes it did find. Only when there are none does it say so and offer "Ask an advisor to find one". `bke_dev_similar` (listing IDs) puts chosen listings first on every kind of page and is never dropped. "Hide Also consider" turns the block off for one listing. The block is cached for each listing. It is rebuilt whenever a published listing's price, bedrooms, area, status or photo changes, a listing is published, unpublished or deleted, or an area or project is renamed. Drafts don't count. The cache reads only the few fields it needs, not each listing's view statistics.

**Unit switcher and breadcrumb.** A unit's page shows a row of the development's homes (1 Bed 6.5M · 2 Bed 13.5M · 3 Bed 18M · All homes). The breadcrumb reads Home / For Sale / Westlands / Golden Mansion / 2 Bedroom Apartment; on phones it shortens to "‹ Golden Mansion". A visitor who came from the site's search or a listings page sees "‹ Back to results" instead, which survives hops between the development's homes. Under the residences table, "See all homes for sale in Westlands" follows the bedroom filter ("Comparing? See other 2-bed homes in Westlands").

**Main area.** A listing tagged with several areas uses its development's area, else the one with the most listings. The Golden Mansion 2-bed, tagged Industrial Area and Westlands, therefore reads Westlands everywhere: the hero, breadcrumb, similar homes and the enquiry email. Set "Main area (slug)" on a listing to choose it by hand.

**Ad landing pages** stay a closed funnel: no search, no similar homes and none of their database queries. They show "Not sure Golden Mansion is the one? Send me 3 alternatives", which opens the enquiry form ("Alternatives request" in the email). Only after the visitor has enquired does the thank-you panel add one link: "While you wait: see other 2-bed homes in Westlands".

**Rolling it out.** `auto_units` (default off) switches the layout on for unit listings for sale without ticking each one. Set it to `true` for all of them, or to a list of area slugs, e.g. `array( 'westlands' )`, to go area by area.

**Tracking.** GA4 / dataLayer events:
- `search_open`, `search_submit` (with the fields and how many the visitor changed), `search_shortlist`;
- `similar_view` (once), `similar_click` (with position and tier), `similar_see_all`;
- `unit_switch`, `units_filter`, `residences_see_all`, `back_to_results`, `lp_after_click`.

The new enquiry sources `alternatives`, `search-shortlist`, `compare` and `find` are named in the lead email.

**Settings** (option `blockke_dev_page`):
- similar homes: `similar_count` (6), `similar_min` (3), `price_band` (0.25), `dev_price_band` (0.35), `neighbours`, `pool_cap` (200), `sim_ttl` (12 hours), `similar_units`;
- search: `search`, `search_url`, `search_min_area_count` (5), `budget_bands`, `beds_mode` (`exact`: links use the home's own bedrooms, so a 5-bed links to 5-bed homes, and the search offers 1 to 5; set `min` if the site search treats bedrooms as "at least", so labels read "2+ bed"); `studio_cat` (`studio-apartments`: the category a Studio search uses);
- navigation and landing pages: `unit_switcher`, `back_to_results`, `lp_alternatives`, `lp_after_link`, `auto_units`.

**Visitors abroad.** When the browser's time zone isn't Nairobi, the phone fields show an international example for that country (for example `+44 7700 900123` in the UK), so diaspora buyers include their country code.

## Install and roll back

1. Open **WPCode → Code Snippets**. To install from this file instead, add a new PHP snippet and paste the file without its first `<?php` line.
2. Set *Insert method* to Auto Insert and *Location* to Run Everywhere. Turn on **Active** and click **Update**.
3. Check an off-plan property: the payment plan calculator replaces the mortgage one, *Features & Amenities* shows the tiles, and *Address* shows *What's nearby*. Then preview a development with `?bke_layout=1`.

To roll back, deactivate the snippet. Every page returns to the theme's own template and calculator. If area pages were on, also save **Settings → Permalinks** once, so the segment URLs (`/property_area/westlands/homes-for-sale/`) stop answering with the theme's area archive.

**Upgrading.** Each version is staged as its own snippet with a lower priority number than the one before, so it runs first: v1.9.1 at priority 3, v1.3 (snippet 42706, now v1.8.2 plus the "v1.9" line) at 4, v1.1 at 5, v1.0 at 10. (v1.2 was never switched on; its snippet became v1.3.) The whole file is wrapped in `if ( ! function_exists( 'blockke_dev_config' ) )`, so whichever version runs first is the one used, and the others skip themselves. To upgrade, switch the new snippet on, and it takes over at once. Then switch the older ones off, or keep the newest of them as a fallback. To go back, switch the new one off.

**Switching the rental pages on (v1.9.1).** v1.9.1 is staged as a new snippet at priority 3, inactive, next to 42706, which stays active as the fallback for two weeks; the enquiries inbox v1.1 is staged the same way at priority 5, next to 42847. Activate the inbox first, then v1.9.1: with every switch off the site is unchanged, except that the three listings in section 4 lose their landing pages. Then write the switches into the option `blockke_dev_page` one at a time, keeping its other keys, in this order: `rent_pages` `true`; `rent_cards` `true`; `classic_inplace` `array( 'rent' )` (soon after `rent_pages`, so visitors without script see the classic rent box); `forms_post` `array( 'rent' )`; `gallery_ssr` `array( 'rent' )`; `listing_jsonld` `array( 'rent' )`; and `redirects` with `redirect_ids` before batch D. ## Enquiries inbox (`block-enquiries-inbox.php`)

- **Why the counts read 0 / 0 / 1 (found 2 Nov 2026):** snippet 44128 carried the `wpcode_location` term "everywhere", so WPCode's run list (option `wpcode_snippets`) held it under `everywhere` although its insert method is shortcode (`auto_insert` 0). Evidence: v2.0.2's stats transient for the current stamp was written one second after a listing save, the v2.0.2 page key was written during a front-page render after the switch to 45145, no v2.1.1 key existed, and the expired v2.0.2 page key was deleted by the next request after 44128 was set to draft.

Separately, to stop 44128 running at all, 44128 is a draft and has no `wpcode_location` term (2 Nov 2026). WPCode only rebuilds `wpcode_snippets` when a snippet is saved or switched in its own screen; a status or term change through the site connection does not rebuild it, and the cached code keeps running until then. Switching 44128 on and then off in WPCode → Code Snippets rebuilds the list without it (a check the same day found the other 28 entries identical to the database, so the rebuild changes nothing else).
- **Rule:** a shortcode-only snippet must have no `wpcode_location` term (45145, 45138 and 44131 have none). Snippet 40708 (Blog hub, shortcode) also carries "everywhere" and is in the run list, so it runs on every request too.
