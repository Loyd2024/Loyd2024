Overview

A leafy Kileleshwa address with the facilities of a private club.

Kileleshwa has long appealed to buyers who want a quiet, tree-lined address with the city close by. Luster Arbor brings a new kind of home to that setting.

The apartments are open-plan and full of natural light, with private balconies and a smart home control panel. Most of what you might otherwise travel for is inside the building: a swim, a workout, a yoga class, a sauna, a quiet hour in the reading lounge, an evening in the cinema room.

For a first-time buyer, a growing family or an investor building a portfolio, there is a wide range of sizes and entry points, starting from KES 4.6M. Occupation is scheduled for Q4 2027.
