Our story

Opened in 2009, on two promises.

Dr. Amara Okafor opened Brightwater on Maple Avenue in 2009. She wanted a practice where every appointment had time in it: time to talk, time to explain, and time to stop if you needed to.

Plenty of people put off the dentist because they are worried about the bill. So the second promise was just as simple: you see the cost before any treatment starts.

The team has grown since then, and the promises haven’t changed. Whether you come every six months or haven’t seen a dentist in years, you won’t get a lecture. We start with a conversation about what you want and what worries you.

How we work

Four habits that keep those promises.

None of them are complicated. They just take time, so we book appointments long enough to keep them.

  • We talk before we start

    A first visit begins with a conversation about your goals and worries, before you sit back in the chair.

  • You see what we see

    Your X-rays and close-up photos go up on the screen beside you, and we explain them in plain words.

  • Options, not pressure

    What needs doing, what can wait, and what each option costs. Then the choice is yours.

  • Raise a hand, we pause

    At any point, for any reason. Numbing gel, headphones and nitrous oxide are there if you want them.

Our office & technology

A screen by the chair, and crowns made on site.

Everything in Suite 200 is there to make your visit clearer, shorter or more comfortable. Ask us about any of it at your appointment.

  • Digital X-rays, low radiation

    Digital X-rays need less radiation than old film X-rays, and they appear on screen in seconds so we can look at them together.

  • Intraoral camera

    A small camera, about the size of a pen, takes close-up photos of your teeth. You see what we see, and decide with us.

  • Same-visit crowns

    We design and mill crowns here in the office, so a crown can often be fitted in one visit, with no temporary crown in between.

  • Nitrous oxide

    Laughing gas to take the edge off, if you want it. Marcus Bell, RDH, is sedation-assist certified.

  • Small comforts

    Noise-canceling headphones, weighted blankets, and numbing gel before any injection. Just ask.

  • Clean, every time

    We follow current infection-control guidance and sterilize instruments for every patient.
