Nervous about coming in?

Choose “I’m nervous — please plan a slower first visit” as your reason, and we’ll leave time to talk before anything starts. No lecture, however long it has been.

What happens next

  1. 1

    We call or text you

    Elena or one of the team gets in touch to offer a time. Nothing is booked until you’ve said yes.

  2. 2

    A reminder 2 days before

    We send it by text, so the time is on your phone.

  3. 3

    Your visit

    New patients: plan on about 75 minutes, and arrive 10 minutes early or fill in your forms online beforehand.
