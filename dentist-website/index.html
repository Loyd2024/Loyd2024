A moment for you

In for 4,
out for 6

Breathe in for a count of four as the circle widens, and out for six as it settles.

  • Numbing gel first

    We numb the gum with gel before any injection, so you feel far less of it.

  • Nitrous oxide

    Laughing gas to take the edge off. Marcus Bell, RDH, is sedation-assist certified.

  • Noise-cancelling headphones

    Bring a playlist or a podcast and tune out the sounds of the room.

  • Weighted blankets

    A steady, gentle weight while you sit back in the chair.

Dental emergency

Toothache, broken tooth or swelling? Call us now.

We keep same-day appointments for emergencies during opening hours. When the office is closed, call our after-hours line.

If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, swelling that is spreading, or a serious injury to the face or jaw, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

    X-rays and photos, on screen

    Low-radiation digital X-rays and close-up photos of your teeth, shown on a screen so you see what we see.

    A clear plan, with costs
