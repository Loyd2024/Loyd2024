Your first visit

Five steps, about 75 minutes.

Every first visit follows the same order, so you always know what comes next. You leave knowing where your teeth stand, what (if anything) needs doing, and what it would cost.

  1. 1

    Arrive 10 minutes early

    Or complete your forms before you come, using the secure link we text you. Bring your photo ID, your insurance card, a list of your medications and any previous X-rays.

  2. 2

    We talk first

    About what you would like from your teeth, and anything that worries you. Nervous? Tell us when you book, and we’ll plan a slower first visit with time to talk before anything starts.

  3. 3

    X-rays and photos, on screen

    Low-radiation digital X-rays and close-up photos of your teeth, shown on a screen so you see what we see.

  4. 4

    Exam and cleaning

    A full exam with Dr. Okafor or Dr. Reyes, including an oral cancer screening, and a cleaning with one of our hygienists. If you need a deep cleaning, we explain why and what it costs before booking it.

  5. 5

    A clear plan, with costs

    What needs doing, what can wait, and what each option costs, with your insurance estimate if you have one. No work begins until you agree to it.

About 75 minutes in all

Questions

What new patients ask us

Short, plain answers to the questions we hear most. Anything else, just ask.

Do you take my insurance?

We are in network with most PPO plans, including Delta Dental PPO, MetLife, Cigna DPPO, Aetna PPO, Guardian and United Concordia. These are examples, so please call (555) 014-2368 to confirm yours. Out-of-network plans are welcome too, and we file the claim for you either way.

How much is a visit without insurance?

Your first visit is $99 if you pay for it yourself. That covers a comprehensive exam, X-rays and a cleaning. A deep cleaning is not included.

For ongoing care without insurance, the Brightwater Membership Plan is $29 a month for adults or $22 a month for kids under 13. It includes two cleanings and exams a year, all routine X-rays, one emergency exam and 15% off other treatment, with no waiting period.

I haven’t been to a dentist in years. Will I be judged?

No. If it has been years since your last visit, you won’t get a lecture. We start with a conversation about what worries you, show you your X-rays on screen, and explain each step before we start. Tell us when you book, and we’ll plan a slower first visit.

Do you offer sedation?

We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas), which takes the edge off while you stay awake and able to talk to us. It is the only sedation we offer. Marcus Bell, RDH, is sedation-assist certified.

We also numb the gum with gel before any injection, and we have noise-cancelling headphones and weighted blankets if you would like them.

Can I stay with my child during their visit?

Yes, you’re welcome to stay in the room. We like to see children by their first birthday, and first visits are short and gentle. Priya Shah, RDH, does most of our kids’ cleanings, and the kids’ membership plan is $22 a month.

What if I need an emergency visit?

Call us. We keep same-day appointments for toothaches, broken teeth, lost fillings or crowns, and swelling. During opening hours, call (555) 014-2368. After hours, call (555) 014-2399.

If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, swelling that is spreading, or a serious injury to the face or jaw, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Do X-rays use much radiation?

Very little. We use digital X-rays, which need far less radiation than older film X-rays, and we take only the ones your dentist needs. You see them on screen with us at the same visit. If you are pregnant or might be, please tell us before any X-rays.

Do you have evening or weekend appointments?

Yes. We’re open until 7:00 pm on Tuesdays, and from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm on the 1st and 3rd Saturday of the month. On weekdays we open at 8:00 am, so an appointment before work is an option too.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Dr. Reyes speaks Spanish, and appointments are available in English or Spanish. Just let us know when you book. Se habla español.

What’s the difference between DDS and DMD?

None that affects your care. DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) and DMD (Doctor of Dental Medicine) are the same degree with the same training; dental schools simply use different names for it. Dr. Okafor is a DDS and Dr. Reyes is a DMD.
