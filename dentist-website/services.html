Emergency dentistry

In pain today? Call us now.

We keep same-day appointments for toothaches, broken teeth, lost fillings or crowns, and swelling during opening hours (Mon–Thu 8:00 am – 5:00 pm, Tue until 7:00 pm, Fri 8:00 am – 2:00 pm, 1st & 3rd Sat 9:00 am – 1:00 pm). When the office is closed, call our after-hours line.

What to do until you see us

Knocked-out adult tooth

Call us now. The tooth has the best chance if you are seen within 30 to 60 minutes. Hold it by the crown (the white part), not the root. Rinse it briefly in water without scrubbing. If you can, gently push it back into its socket and bite on a clean cloth to hold it in place. If not, keep it in a cup of milk and bring it with you.

A knocked-out baby tooth should not be put back. Just call us.

Broken or chipped tooth

Rinse your mouth with warm water and hold a cold compress against your cheek to keep swelling down. Keep any pieces you find, and call us.

Lost crown or filling

Keep the crown and bring it with you; we can often fix it back in place. Please don’t glue it back on yourself. Until then, chew on the other side.
