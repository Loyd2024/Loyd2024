FAQ

Good questions.

Where exactly is Sensei?

On Greenwood Drive in Nyali, Mombasa, on the beachfront itself: 1.89 acres with 67 metres of direct beach frontage.

Who is developing it?

Sensei is developed by Amouage Development Limited, working with HassConsult. Ask us for the developer's track record and the project documents.

What can I buy?

One, two and three bedroom apartments, and duplexes from one to four bedrooms: 111 residences on 1.89 acres.

How much are the residences, and how do I pay?

Residences start from KES 24M, with payment spread over 30 months. Request the price list and payment schedule for the current price of each layout, as launch pricing changes as units sell.

What amenities are there?

An infinity lagoon, courtyards and seaside lookout, a fully fitted gym, a film lounge and Ocean Pavilion, a work studio, beach cabanas and lounge islands, a sand play park and kids' tide pool, and curated resident events.

Can I let my residence as a holiday home?

Many buyers plan to. Ask for the development's letting and management terms, and use the estimator above to run your own numbers.

Can I buy from abroad?

Yes. Kenyans in the diaspora and foreign nationals can buy apartments in Kenya, and the process can largely be handled remotely with an advisor.

When will it be complete?

Completion is scheduled for Q3 2029. The launch pack includes the construction timeline.
